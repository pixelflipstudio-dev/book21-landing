The book

Every hand has a right play. Most people at the table don't know it.

It's called basic strategy, and the casino's edge shrinks to almost nothing when you use it. Book 21 turns the chart into habit, one hand at a time, so you aren't guessing with real chips in front of you.

A real table

Full hands from a real shoe.

Other players fill the seats, the dealer checks for blackjack, and the shoe runs down like it does in the casino. Pick the rules on your cruise ship or in Vegas and practice that exact game.

A coach on every decision

Graded on the call, never the cards.

A right play that loses is still right. The coach tells you what the book says, why, and what the other play would have cost you over the long run. A miss is just a spot to practice.

Drills

Make the play. Find the slip. Add it up.

Fast drills for the hands that trip people up. Tricky totals, soft hands and pairs, spotting the mistake in someone else's hand, and keeping count of the cards.

Progress you earn

Streaks and badges for playing it right.

Rewards come from good decisions only. Never from winning, betting big or luck. Get to 25 in a row by the book and the dealer starts to notice.
